Why "features" not "services": "Services" signals a transaction. "Features" signals a platform. 5/5 competitors use outcome-based, platform-framing language. The target customer (skeptics) will read "services" as commercial and disengage.

Why "about" before "testimonials": For a trust-first academic platform, establishing who built this (credentials, mission) must precede asking the visitor to believe third-party testimonials. Present in 4/5 competitors.

Why "partners": Institutional logos (SBL, universities, media outlets) are the single highest-ROI trust signal in this niche. Present in 4/5 competitors and 4/6 inspiration sites.

04 — Animation Vocabulary

Motion in Flutter

The inspiration sites use controlled revelation — not entertainment. Elements appear intentionally. This maps directly to David Blaine's aesthetic: the reveal is yours to witness, not pushed at you. All animations should feel chosen, not automatic.

05 — Guest Flow

From Landing to Conversion

Free content first. Trust before ask. Membership as a voluntary act of support — not a paywall. Derived from 4/5 competitors and 5/6 inspiration sites.

1
Hero — "Explore the Texts"
Full-bleed dark hero. Large serif headline. Single CTA. Trust subtext: tagline + one scholar credential. No pricing. No account wall. The visitor can proceed without deciding anything.
CTA: "Explore the Texts"
2
Free Content — Browse without account
Visitor reads a comparison, views a text, follows a citation. Zero friction. The content is the CTA. 4/5 competitors and all 6 inspiration sites gate nothing at this stage. The content earns the next step.
3
Soft prompt — "Track your reading"
After meaningful engagement (3+ page views, or reaching a key comparison page), a subtle inline prompt: "Track which texts you've read — create a free account." Lightweight. No modal. No countdown.
CTA: "Start tracking — free"
4
Account dashboard — premium features visible
Free account unlocks tracking, highlights, and saved comparisons. Premium features (annotation sets, citation export, cross-tradition comparison packs) are visible but gated — not hidden. Modeled on Ehrman Blog's tier transparency.
5
Conversion — membership or one-time support
Positioned as "support scholarly independence" — Ehrman Blog's 100%-to-charity model proves this framing converts skeptics. Not a paywall. A voluntary contribution with clear access benefits.
CTA: "Support the research"
06 — Conflicts & Gaps

What Needs Attention

Items derived from the brand brief, inspo URL analysis, and competitor blueprint. Ordered by severity.

Severity Issue Finding & Recommendation
🔴 Critical No blog module 4/5 competitors and 4/6 inspo sites use long-form articles as primary SEO and trust engine. The entire keyword space ("canon formation", "bible translation history") is informational — won by content depth. Without blog, Tracking Faith cannot rank or build long-term trust. Add "blog" to MODULES and HOME_SECTIONS.
🔴 Critical No scholar credential / about section 5/5 competitors anchor trust with a named person (institution + title) before the CTA. For a platform targeting skeptics, unattributed content = zero credibility. An "about" section with contributor profiles is table stakes. Add "about" to HOME_SECTIONS and implement contributor credential display.
🟡 Moderate "services" label mismatch Retail/commercial framing ("services") creates cognitive dissonance for the target customer. Replace with "features" in HOME_SECTIONS. 5/5 competitors use platform-framing language, never "services."
🟡 Moderate No partner/institutional logos Institutional logos are the highest-ROI trust signal in this niche. 4/5 competitors use them. Flyward.com (inspiration) uses IATA + partner logos as primary trust anchor. Add "partners" to HOME_SECTIONS.
🟡 Moderate "peaceful" tension vs. David Blaine PERSONALITY includes "peaceful" — correct for the scholarly container (no sales noise, no urgency). But the David Blaine celebrity reference requires "moments of controlled dramatic revelation." The design must be quiet until it speaks, then speak with force. Ensure the Flutter personality preset allows atmospheric dark sections and bold typographic reveals.
🟢 Aligned Color approach (dark + warm gold) 5/6 inspo sites support dark backgrounds. Warm gold aligns with manuscript/parchment aesthetic for "Ancient." No conflicts across inspo URL set.
🟢 Aligned Cormorant + Inter pairing Industry-validated for academic knowledge platforms. Aligns with "Trusted Ancient Modern." No conflict with inspo URL aesthetic.
🟢 Aligned faq, testimonials, cta These sections are appropriate and correctly positioned. Testimonials must be scholar-attributed (not star ratings) — REVIEWS_ENABLED=false is correct.
